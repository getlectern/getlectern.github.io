Lectern Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Lectern is a note-taking and flashcard app. We built it so your notes stay yours.

What we collect

Very little. Lectern has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we never sell personal information.

If you create a Lectern account (Sign in with Apple or Google, optional), we store your name, your email address (or the private relay address Apple gives us), which sign-in you used, the kind of device you last used, and an app-specific iCloud identifier that tells us which Apple Account your notes sync through. This lets you use the same account on your iPad, iPhone and the website. The identifier is created by Apple for Lectern only and doesn't reveal your Apple ID.

Account data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. You can delete your account at any time in Lectern's Settings or on the account page of our website; this deletes the stored data for good.

Where your notes are stored

Your notebooks, pages, drawings, slides and flashcards are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, Apple syncs them between your own devices using your private iCloud database. We cannot see this data, and it is never copied to your Lectern account. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Lecture recordings

When you record a lecture, the audio is saved with your notes. Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition in on-device mode, and notes are written by Apple Intelligence on your device where available. Audio and transcripts are never sent to us or to any other server, apart from syncing to your own iCloud. Please follow your school's rules about recording lectures.

The Hub (sharing with other students)

The Hub lets you download flashcard decks, notes, study guides and themes, and share your own. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share. When you share, the item's title, contents, category and the nickname you type are stored in Lectern's public iCloud database, which Apple hosts. Anyone using Lectern can see and download it. iCloud also stores an anonymous ID for your iCloud account with the item, so you can remove it later and so other people can block you; we cannot see your name or Apple Account from it.

You can remove anything you shared at any time from the item in the Hub. If you report an item, the report (the item and the reason you picked) is stored in the same database so we can review it, normally within 24 hours. We remove items that break the community rules and can stop accounts that repeatedly break them. Blocking an author is saved only on your device.

Community rules: no offensive, hateful, sexual or violent content, no harassment, no spam or advertising, and nothing you don't have the right to share, such as copied textbook pages.

Files you import

PDF slides, images and Anki decks you add are read on your device and saved with your notes. They are not uploaded to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Lectern does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
