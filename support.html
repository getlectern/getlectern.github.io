Lectern Support

Need help? Email lecternapp.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Making flashcards

Write a term in bold, then a colon and its meaning. Or select words and tap Card to make a fill-in-the-blank card. Cards show up under Flashcards.

Lecture slides

Turn on Lecture, then choose a PDF. PowerPoint or Keynote decks need to be exported as PDF first. Tap Link slide to tag a note with the slide that's showing.

Importing from Anki

In Anki, export a deck as an .apkg file (include scheduling information to keep your progress). In Lectern, tap + in the sidebar, then Import Anki deck.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore a subscription on a new device, tap Restore purchases in Lectern's Settings.

Sync

Notes sync through iCloud between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Make sure iCloud Drive is on in Settings.
